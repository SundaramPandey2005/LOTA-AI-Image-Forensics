# LOTA: Mathematical & Methodological Research Notes

**Paper Title**: LOTA: Bit-Planes Guided AI-Generated Image Detection  
**Authors**: Hongsong Wang, Renxi Cheng, Yang Zhang, Chaolei Han, Jie Gui  
**Venue**: ICCV 2025 (CVF Open Access)  
**Official Repository**: `https://github.com/hongsong-wang/LOTA`

---

## 1. Core Problem & Paradigm Shift
- **Limitation of Existing Error Extraction**: Diffusion-reconstruction detectors (DIRE, SeDID, LaRE²) require iterative DDIM sampling ($20\text{ to }100$ steps), introducing random noise and severe latency ($0.26\text{s to }2.0\text{s}$ per image).
- **Core Hypothesis**: Lower bit planes of digital images preserve imperceptible micro-noise. Natural camera images have regular noise distributions originating from optical sensors; synthetic images generated by neural networks fail to accurately model physical sensor noise or produce chaotic high-frequency distribution discrepancies in low bit planes.
- **LOTA Advantage**: Single-step error extraction at $1.52\text{ ms}$, total inference in $4.00\text{ ms}$, parameter footprint of $23.6\text{M}$ (ResNet-50).

## 2. Mathematical Formulations

### 2.1 Bit-Plane Decomposition & Low-Bit Composition
For each color channel $c \in \{R, G, B\}$:
$$\bm{x^c} = \sum_{k=0}^7 2^k \cdot \bm{x_k^c}, \quad \bm{x_k^c} \in \{0, 1\} \quad \text{--- Eq. (1)}$$

Combining the $K=3$ lowest-order bit planes ($k=0, 1, 2$):
$$\bm{z^c} = 2^2 \cdot \bm{x_2^c} + 2^1 \cdot \bm{x_1^c} + \bm{x_0^c} \quad \text{--- Eq. (2)}$$
where $z^c(i, j) \in \{0, 1, \dots, 7\}$.

### 2.2 Normalization Strategies
1. **Thresholding (Default LOTA)**:
   $$\tilde{z}_{i,j}^c = \begin{cases} 0, & \text{if } z_{i,j}^c = 0 \\ 255, & \text{if } z_{i,j}^c > 0 \end{cases} \quad \text{--- Eq. (4)}$$
2. **Scaling (LOTA-scl.)**:
   $$\tilde{\bm{z}}^c = 255 \cdot \frac{\bm{z}^c - \bm{z_{\min}^c}}{\bm{z_{\max}^c} - \bm{z_{\min}^c}} \quad \text{--- Eq. (3)}$$

### 2.3 Maximum Gradient Patch Selection (MGPS)
The normalized noise image $\tilde{z}$ is resized to $256 \times 256$ and partitioned into $32 \times 32$ candidate patches $\tilde{z}_p$.
The divergence score $g_p$ is computed via 4 directional 2D convolution kernels:
$$\bm{g_x} = \begin{bmatrix} -1 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \bm{g_y} = \begin{bmatrix} -1 \\ 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \bm{g_{xy}} = \begin{bmatrix} -1 & 0 \\ 0 & 1 \end{bmatrix}, \quad \bm{g_{yx}} = \begin{bmatrix} 0 & -1 \\ 1 & 0 \end{bmatrix}$$
$$g_p = \|\tilde{\bm{z}}_p * \bm{g_x}\|_1 + \|\tilde{\bm{z}}_p * \bm{g_y}\|_1 + \|\tilde{\bm{z}}_p * \bm{g_{xy}}\|_1 + \|\tilde{\bm{z}}_p * \bm{g_{yx}}\|_1 \quad \text{--- Eq. (5)}$$
$$\tilde{\bm{z}}_{p^*} = \arg\max_p g_p \quad \text{--- Eq. (6)}$$

### 2.4 Classification Architectures
1. **Noise-Based Classifier (NBC)**:
   - Input: Selected patch $\tilde{z}_{p^*}$ bilinearly resized from $32 \times 32 \to 256 \times 256$.
   - Backbone: Pretrained ResNet-50 with final linear binary projection.
2. **Noise-Guided Classifier (NGC)**:
   - Raw image $x \to$ ResNet-50 $\to$ feature map $\tilde{x} \to Q, K, V$.
   - Selected patch $\tilde{z}_{p^*} \to$ Flatten $\to$ Linear $\to E$.
   - Spatial Cross-Attention:
     $$U = \text{softmax}\left(\frac{QK^T}{\sqrt{d_k}} + E\right) V \quad \text{--- Eq. (7)}$$
   - Followed by multi-head attention and linear binary classifier.

## 3. Paper Results Summary
- **Average Accuracy on GenImage**: 98.9% (vs ESSP 87.0%, LaRE² 79.4%, DIRE 73.4%, ResNet-50 66.9%).
- **Cross-Domain Generalization**:
  - BigGAN-trained $\to$ SD v1.5: 99.9%, GLIDE: 98.2%, VQDM: 99.4%.
  - SD v1.5-trained $\to$ BigGAN: 100%, Midjourney: 93.1%, GLIDE: 100%.
- **Key Ablation Insights**:
  - 3 low bit planes ($K=3$) is optimal (96.8% avg vs 91.5% for 1-bit and 86.4% for 6-bits).
  - $32 \times 32$ patch size achieves optimal balance between noise focus and context (96.8% vs 92.7% for $16 \times 16$ and 90.8% for $64 \times 64$).
  - Max gradient selection outperforms Random (93.1%) and Min gradient (89.6%).
